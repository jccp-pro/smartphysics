How To Use

1 · CAL
Hold the phone still, well away from metal, in the orientation you’ll scan with. Tap CALIBRATE: the app measures μ and σ of |B|.
2 · DETECT
Tap DETECT, then sweep the phone slowly over the area. When |B − μ| exceeds kσ it beeps — higher pitch and faster beeps mean a bigger deviation.
TIP
Don’t rotate the phone while scanning: phone magnetometers carry a small built-in offset, so rotating alone can change |B|. Translate, don’t twist.
WHERE
The sensor usually sits near the top of the phone. Find it by moving a small magnet or steel screw around the case.
RANGE
Expect a few cm for steel screws or nails, more for large iron objects. Aluminium, copper, gold aren’t ferromagnetic — this detector won’t see them.
